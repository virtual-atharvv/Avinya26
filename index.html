About the Fest

Two Worlds.
One Stage.

अvinya '26 is the flagship techno-cultural fest of the Department of CSE (AIML) at Bansal Institute of Engineering & Technology, Lucknow — where the precision of code collides with the pulse of culture.

Across two electrifying days, hackathons and shark tanks share the arena with open mics and dance floors. Whether you compile neural nets or choreograph narratives, अvinya is your stage. यहाँ हर किसी के लिए कुछ न कुछ है।

The Department of CSE (AIML) at BIET trains engineers at the frontier of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — and अvinya is where that training comes alive.
